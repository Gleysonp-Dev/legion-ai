# Email Writer

> Legion procedure `email-writer` · pack **Writing & Communication** (`legion-writing`)

## When to use
Write clear professional emails: requests, follow-ups, updates, apologies and negotiations. Use for any important email.

## Procedure
1. Identify the goal and recipient.
2. Put the ask in the first lines.
3. Give context briefly.
4. Make next steps explicit.
5. Match tone to relationship.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Ask is clear
- [ ] concise

## Output
Email draft.
