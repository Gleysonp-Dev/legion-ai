# Tone Adapter

> Legion procedure `tone-adapter` · pack **Writing & Communication** (`legion-writing`)

## When to use
Adapt a text to a different tone or audience (formal, casual, technical, executive, friendly) without changing facts. Use when the same message must reach different readers.

## Procedure
1. Identify target audience and tone.
2. Adjust vocabulary and sentence length.
3. Adjust detail level.
4. Keep facts identical.
5. Show side-by-side if useful.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Facts unchanged
- [ ] tone consistent

## Output
Adapted text.
