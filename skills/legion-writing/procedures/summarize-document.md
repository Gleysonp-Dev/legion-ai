# Summarize Document

> Legion procedure `summarize-document` · pack **Writing & Communication** (`legion-writing`)

## When to use
Summarise long documents, reports, transcripts or threads into key points, decisions and action items. Use to digest content quickly.

## Procedure
1. Identify document type and reader need.
2. Extract main points.
3. List decisions and action items.
4. Keep important numbers.
5. Offer a one-line TL;DR.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] No invented facts
- [ ] numbers preserved

## Output
Summary.
