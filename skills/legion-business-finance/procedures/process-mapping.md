# Process Mapping

> Legion procedure `process-mapping` · pack **Business & Finance** (`legion-business-finance`)

## When to use
Map business processes (as-is and to-be) with swimlanes, bottlenecks and improvements. Use for operational improvement.

## Procedure
1. Interview or read documentation.
2. Map as-is steps and owners.
3. Identify bottlenecks.
4. Design to-be.
5. Quantify improvement.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Owners per step
- [ ] bottlenecks quantified

## Output
Process maps.
