# KPI Definition

> Legion procedure `kpi-definition` · pack **Business & Finance** (`legion-business-finance`)

## When to use
Define business KPIs with formulas, data sources, owners, targets and review cadence. Use to manage performance.

## Procedure
1. Link KPIs to goals.
2. Define formulas.
3. Identify sources.
4. Assign owners.
5. Set targets.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every KPI has formula and owner

## Output
KPI catalogue.
