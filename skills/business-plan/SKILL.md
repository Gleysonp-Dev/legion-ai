# Business Plan

> Legion skill · category **Business & Finance**

## When to use
Write a business plan: problem, solution, market, model, go-to-market, operations, team, financials and risks. Use for starting a business or seeking funding.

## Procedure
1. Describe problem and solution.
2. Analyse market and competitors.
3. Define business model and pricing.
4. Plan go-to-market and operations.
5. Project financials and risks.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Assumptions explicit
- [ ] financials consistent

## Output
Business plan document.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `business-analyst`, `financial-analyst`, `project-manager`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
