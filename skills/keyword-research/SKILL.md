# Keyword Research

> Legion skill · category **Marketing & Growth**

## When to use
Research keywords by intent, volume and difficulty and map them to pages. Use for content and SEO planning.

## Procedure
1. Brainstorm seed topics.
2. Expand with tools or search suggestions.
3. Classify intent.
4. Cluster by topic.
5. Map clusters to pages.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] One primary intent per page

## Output
Keyword map.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `growth-strategist`, `seo-specialist`, `social-media-manager`, `content-strategist`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
