# Market Sizing

> Legion skill · category **Research & Analysis**

## When to use
Estimate TAM, SAM and SOM with top-down and bottom-up methods and explicit assumptions. Use for business plans and pitches.

## Procedure
1. Define the market and customer.
2. Top-down from industry data.
3. Bottom-up from customers × price.
4. Reconcile.
5. List assumptions with sources.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Both methods
- [ ] assumptions sourced

## Output
Market sizing table.
