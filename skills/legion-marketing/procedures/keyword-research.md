# Keyword Research

> Legion procedure `keyword-research` · pack **Marketing & Growth** (`legion-marketing`)

## When to use
Research keywords by intent, volume and difficulty and map them to pages. Use for content and SEO planning.

## Procedure
1. Brainstorm seed topics.
2. Expand with tools or search suggestions.
3. Classify intent.
4. Cluster by topic.
5. Map clusters to pages.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] One primary intent per page

## Output
Keyword map.
