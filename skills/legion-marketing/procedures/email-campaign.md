# Email Campaign

> Legion procedure `email-campaign` · pack **Marketing & Growth** (`legion-marketing`)

## When to use
Write email campaigns and sequences (welcome, nurture, launch, re-engagement) with subject lines and CTAs. Use for email marketing.

## Procedure
1. Define goal and audience segment.
2. Plan sequence timing.
3. Write subject lines and preview text variants.
4. Write body with one CTA.
5. Plan A/B tests.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] One CTA per email
- [ ] unsubscribe included

## Output
Email sequence.
