# Growth Experiments

> Legion procedure `growth-experiments` · pack **Marketing & Growth** (`legion-marketing`)

## When to use
Design growth experiments with hypotheses, metrics, sample sizes and ICE scores. Use to grow systematically.

## Procedure
1. Identify funnel bottleneck.
2. Generate hypotheses.
3. Score with ICE.
4. Design experiment with metric and duration.
5. Plan analysis.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Hypothesis falsifiable
- [ ] metric defined

## Output
Experiment backlog.
