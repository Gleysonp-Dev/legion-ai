# SEO Audit

> Legion procedure `seo-audit` · pack **Marketing & Growth** (`legion-marketing`)

## When to use
Audit a website's SEO: technical issues, on-page factors, content gaps and quick wins. Use to increase organic traffic.

## Procedure
1. Crawl the site for indexing, status codes and duplicates.
2. Check titles, metas, headings and structured data.
3. Check speed and mobile usability.
4. Find content gaps vs competitors.
5. Prioritise fixes.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Prioritised by impact
- [ ] technical and content covered

## Output
SEO audit report.
