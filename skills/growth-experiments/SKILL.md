# Growth Experiments

> Legion skill · category **Marketing & Growth**

## When to use
Design growth experiments with hypotheses, metrics, sample sizes and ICE scores. Use to grow systematically.

## Procedure
1. Identify funnel bottleneck.
2. Generate hypotheses.
3. Score with ICE.
4. Design experiment with metric and duration.
5. Plan analysis.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Hypothesis falsifiable
- [ ] metric defined

## Output
Experiment backlog.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `growth-strategist`, `seo-specialist`, `social-media-manager`, `content-strategist`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
