# Deep Research

> Legion procedure `deep-research` · pack **Research & Analysis** (`legion-research`)

## When to use
Research a question in depth using multiple sources, compare evidence and produce a cited report with confidence levels. Use for complex questions.

## Procedure
1. Break the question into sub-questions.
2. Search each one; prefer primary sources.
3. Extract claims with citations and dates.
4. Compare and resolve conflicts.
5. Write findings with confidence and gaps.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every claim cited
- [ ] conflicts noted

## Output
Research report.
