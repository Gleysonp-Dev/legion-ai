# Competitor Teardown

> Legion procedure `competitor-teardown` · pack **Research & Analysis** (`legion-research`)

## When to use
Analyse a single competitor deeply: product, pricing, positioning, funnel, tech and weaknesses. Use to understand a rival.

## Procedure
1. Collect public information.
2. Analyse product and UX.
3. Analyse pricing and packaging.
4. Analyse marketing and positioning.
5. Summarise strengths and weaknesses.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Public sources only
- [ ] cited

## Output
Teardown report.
