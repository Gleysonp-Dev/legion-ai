# Interview Synthesis

> Legion procedure `interview-synthesis` · pack **Research & Analysis** (`legion-research`)

## When to use
Synthesise interview notes or transcripts into themes, quotes and insights. Use after customer or expert interviews.

## Procedure
1. Tag notes by topic.
2. Cluster into themes.
3. Count frequency.
4. Select quotes.
5. Derive insights.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Quotes attributed anonymously
- [ ] themes counted

## Output
Synthesis report.
