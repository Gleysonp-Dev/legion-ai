# Source Evaluation

> Legion procedure `source-evaluation` · pack **Research & Analysis** (`legion-research`)

## When to use
Evaluate the credibility of sources: authority, methodology, bias, recency and corroboration. Use when sources conflict.

## Procedure
1. Identify author and publisher.
2. Assess methodology.
3. Check bias and funding.
4. Check recency.
5. Check corroboration.
