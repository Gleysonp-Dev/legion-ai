# Trend Analysis

> Legion procedure `trend-analysis` · pack **Research & Analysis** (`legion-research`)

## When to use
Identify and assess trends in an industry or technology with evidence and implications. Use for strategy.

## Procedure
1. Collect signals from news, reports and data.
2. Group into trends.
3. Assess momentum and maturity.
4. Analyse implications.
5. Recommend actions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Evidence for each trend

## Output
Trend report.
