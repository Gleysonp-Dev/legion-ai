# License Compliance

> Legion procedure `license-compliance` · pack **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)** (`legion-legal-privacy`)

## When to use
Check open-source licence compatibility for a project and its dependencies. Use before distributing software.

## Procedure
1. List dependencies and licences.
2. Identify copyleft obligations.
3. Check compatibility.
4. Generate notices file.
5. Flag issues.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Notices generated
- [ ] conflicts flagged

## Output
Licence report.
