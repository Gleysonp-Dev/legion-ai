# Privacy Policy

> Legion procedure `privacy-policy` · pack **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)** (`legion-legal-privacy`)

## When to use
Draft a privacy policy reflecting actual data practices, LGPD/GDPR/CCPA disclosures and user rights, marked for legal review. Use when launching an app or site.

## Procedure
1. Inventory data collected, purposes, sharing and retention.
2. Map legal bases.
3. Describe user rights and how to exercise them.
4. Write in plain language.
5. Mark as draft for lawyer review.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Matches real data practices
- [ ] review notice

## Output
Privacy policy draft.
