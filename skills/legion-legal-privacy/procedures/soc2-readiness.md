# SOC 2 Readiness

> Legion procedure `soc2-readiness` · pack **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)** (`legion-legal-privacy`)

## When to use
Prepare for SOC 2: control matrix, policies, evidence collection and gap remediation. Use for B2B SaaS selling to enterprises.

## Procedure
1. Define scope and criteria.
2. Map controls.
3. Draft policies.
4. Plan evidence.
5. Remediate gaps.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every control has owner and evidence

## Output
Readiness plan.
