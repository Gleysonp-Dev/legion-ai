# Terms Of Service

> Legion procedure `terms-of-service` · pack **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)** (`legion-legal-privacy`)

## When to use
Draft terms of service covering usage, accounts, payments, IP, liability and termination, marked for legal review. Use for apps and SaaS.

## Procedure
1. Identify business model and risks.
2. Draft sections.
3. Use plain language.
4. Align with privacy policy.
5. Mark for review.

Terms draft.
