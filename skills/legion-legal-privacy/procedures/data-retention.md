# Data Retention

> Legion procedure `data-retention` · pack **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)** (`legion-legal-privacy`)

## When to use
Define and implement data retention and deletion schedules. Use to reduce risk and comply with regulations.

## Procedure
1. Inventory data types.
2. Set retention periods with reasons.
3. Implement automated deletion.
4. Handle backups.
5. Document schedule.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Automated deletion in place

## Output
Retention schedule and jobs.
