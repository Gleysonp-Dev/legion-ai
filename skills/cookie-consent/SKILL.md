# Cookie Consent

> Legion skill · category **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)**

## When to use
Implement cookie consent with categories, blocking before consent and records of consent. Use for websites with analytics or ads.

## Procedure
1. Inventory cookies and scripts.
2. Categorise.
3. Block non-essential until consent.
4. Store consent records.
5. Allow changes.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Non-essential blocked before consent

## Output
Consent implementation.
