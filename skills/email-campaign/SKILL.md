# Email Campaign

> Legion skill · category **Marketing & Growth**

## When to use
Write email campaigns and sequences (welcome, nurture, launch, re-engagement) with subject lines and CTAs. Use for email marketing.

## Procedure
1. Define goal and audience segment.
2. Plan sequence timing.
3. Write subject lines and preview text variants.
4. Write body with one CTA.
5. Plan A/B tests.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] One CTA per email
- [ ] unsubscribe included

## Output
Email sequence.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `growth-strategist`, `seo-specialist`, `social-media-manager`, `content-strategist`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
