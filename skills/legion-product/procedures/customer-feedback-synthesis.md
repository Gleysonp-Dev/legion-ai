# Customer Feedback Synthesis

> Legion procedure `customer-feedback-synthesis` · pack **Product Management** (`legion-product`)

## When to use
Synthesise customer feedback from tickets, reviews and interviews into themes, counts and recommendations. Use to decide what to fix or build.

## Procedure
1. Collect feedback sources.
2. Code each item into themes.
3. Count and weight by customer value.
4. Quote representative examples.
5. Recommend actions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Themes quantified
- [ ] quotes included

## Output
Feedback synthesis.
