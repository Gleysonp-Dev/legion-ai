# Competitive Analysis

> Legion procedure `competitive-analysis` · pack **Product Management** (`legion-product`)

## When to use
Compare competitors' features, pricing, positioning and reviews to find opportunities. Use for product strategy.

## Procedure
1. Identify direct and indirect competitors.
2. Build a feature and pricing matrix.
3. Summarise positioning and reviews.
4. Find gaps.
5. Recommend differentiation.

- [ ] Sources cited with dates

## Output
Competitive analysis.
