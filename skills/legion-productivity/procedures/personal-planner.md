# Personal Planner

> Legion procedure `personal-planner` · pack **Productivity & Automation** (`legion-productivity`)

## When to use
Create a weekly plan or schedule balancing priorities, deadlines, energy and routines. Use for time management.

## Procedure
1. List commitments and deadlines.
2. Prioritise tasks.
3. Block time.
4. Add buffers.
5. Review at week end.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Realistic load
- [ ] buffers included

## Output
Weekly plan.
