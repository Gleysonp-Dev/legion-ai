# Meeting Notes

> Legion skill · category **Productivity & Automation**

## When to use
Turn meeting transcripts or notes into summaries with decisions, action items, owners and dates. Use after meetings.

## Procedure
1. Identify participants and purpose.
2. Summarise discussion.
3. List decisions.
4. List action items with owners and dates.
5. List open questions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Actions owned and dated

## Output
Meeting summary.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `operations-automator`, `presentation-designer`, `project-manager`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.

## Personalisation
If `~/.legion/memory/profile.md` exists, apply the user's recorded preferences (language, tone, formats, stack) to this procedure.
