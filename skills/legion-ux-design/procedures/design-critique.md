# Design Critique

> Legion procedure `design-critique` · pack **UX, UI & Accessibility** (`legion-ux-design`)

## When to use
Critique a design or screenshot constructively: goals, hierarchy, clarity, consistency, accessibility. Use for feedback on mockups.

## Procedure
1. Clarify the design's goal.
2. Assess hierarchy and clarity.
3. Check consistency and accessibility.
4. Note strengths.
5. Give prioritised suggestions.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Feedback tied to goals
- [ ] prioritised

## Output
Critique notes.
