# Persona Builder

> Legion procedure `persona-builder` · pack **UX, UI & Accessibility** (`legion-ux-design`)

## When to use
Create evidence-based personas or jobs-to-be-done profiles from research data. Use to align a team on who the user is.

## Procedure
1. Gather research data.
2. Cluster users by goals and behaviours.
3. Write personas with goals, pains and context.
4. Mark assumptions.
5. Use JTBD statements.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Based on evidence
- [ ] assumptions marked

## Output
Persona profiles.
