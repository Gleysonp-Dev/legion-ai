# SOC 2 Readiness

> Legion skill · category **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)**

## When to use
Prepare for SOC 2: control matrix, policies, evidence collection and gap remediation. Use for B2B SaaS selling to enterprises.

## Procedure
1. Define scope and criteria.
2. Map controls.
3. Draft policies.
4. Plan evidence.
5. Remediate gaps.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every control has owner and evidence

## Output
Readiness plan.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `legal-researcher`, `privacy-engineer`, `compliance-analyst`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
