# Contract Review

> Legion skill · category **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)**

## When to use
Review a contract for key terms, obligations, risks and unusual clauses, summarised in plain language (not legal advice). Use before signing.

## Procedure
1. Identify parties, term and payment.
2. Summarise obligations.
3. Flag risky clauses.
4. List questions for the counterparty.
5. Recommend lawyer review for high stakes.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Risky clauses flagged
- [ ] not-legal-advice notice

## Output
Contract summary and issue list.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `legal-researcher`, `privacy-engineer`, `compliance-analyst`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
