# LGPD GDPR Compliance

> Legion skill · category **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)**

## When to use
Assess LGPD/GDPR compliance technically: data inventory, legal bases, consent, rights requests, DPAs and breach process. Use when processing personal data.

## Procedure
1. Build data inventory.
2. Check legal bases and consent.
3. Check rights request processes.
4. Check vendors and DPAs.
5. Check breach procedure.

## Output
Compliance gap report.
