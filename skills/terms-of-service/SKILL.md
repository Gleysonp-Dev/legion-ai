# Terms Of Service

> Legion skill · category **Legal, Privacy & Compliance (not legal advice)**

## When to use
Draft terms of service covering usage, accounts, payments, IP, liability and termination, marked for legal review. Use for apps and SaaS.

## Procedure
1. Identify business model and risks.
2. Draft sections.
3. Use plain language.
4. Align with privacy policy.
5. Mark for review.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Review notice
- [ ] consistent with product

## Output
Terms draft.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `legal-researcher`, `privacy-engineer`, `compliance-analyst`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.
