# Deep Research

> Legion skill · category **Research & Analysis**

## When to use
Research a question in depth using multiple sources, compare evidence and produce a cited report with confidence levels. Use for complex questions.

## Procedure
1. Break the question into sub-questions.
2. Search each one; prefer primary sources.
3. Extract claims with citations and dates.
4. Compare and resolve conflicts.
5. Write findings with confidence and gaps.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every claim cited
- [ ] conflicts noted

## Output
Research report.

## Works well with
Legion agents: `research-analyst`, `market-researcher`. For large or multi-part work, hand the task to the `legion` skill so the conductor can split it across agents.

## Personalisation
If `~/.legion/memory/profile.md` exists, apply the user's recorded preferences (language, tone, formats, stack) to this procedure.
