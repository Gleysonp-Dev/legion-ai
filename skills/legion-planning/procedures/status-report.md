# Status Report

> Legion procedure `status-report` · pack **Planning & Delivery** (`legion-planning`)

## When to use
Write a concise project status report: progress, next steps, blockers, decisions needed and health. Use for weekly updates to stakeholders.

## Procedure
1. Collect what was completed since the last report with evidence (links, demos).
2. List next steps with owners and dates.
3. Call out blockers and the decision or help needed.
4. Rate health (green/yellow/red) with a one-line reason.
5. Keep it readable in under two minutes.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Leads with the headline
- [ ] blockers have an ask
- [ ] no jargon

## Output
A status update ready to paste into email or chat.
