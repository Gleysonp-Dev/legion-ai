# Retrospective

> Legion procedure `retrospective` · pack **Planning & Delivery** (`legion-planning`)

## When to use
Run a retrospective: what went well, what didn't, root causes and concrete actions. Use after a sprint, project or incident.

## Procedure
1. Gather facts first: timeline, metrics, events.
2. Collect what went well and what was hard without blame.
3. Group items into themes and find root causes with "5 whys".
4. Choose at most 3 actions with owners and due dates.
5. Check last retro's actions for follow-through.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Actions are specific and owned
- [ ] tone is blameless

## Output
Retro notes with themes and action items.
